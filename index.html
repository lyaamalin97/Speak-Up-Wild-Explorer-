💡 How to Play

  1. Create your explorer and choose your favourite look.
  2. Choose Easy, Intermediate, or Hard.
  3. Pick any habitat on the world map.
  4. Listen to Kiki's clue and speak your answer.
  5. If the microphone is unavailable, type your answer instead.
  6. Use hints, sentence frames, and model answers when you need help.
  7. Discover all 20 animals to earn your MASTER EXPLORER certificate!
🎤 About the microphone

Speech recognition uses the browser's Web Speech API and is not available in every browser. Chrome or Edge may work best. Microphone permission is required, and HTTPS or localhost is generally needed. If unsupported, the game will say so and typed answers remain available. Recognition text is checked against simple local rules; it is not an accurate pronunciation score.

🔧 Teacher Settings (teacher use)

You can edit names, clues, descriptions, and sentence frames. Keep the JSON structure valid. Custom changes are saved in this browser only.
